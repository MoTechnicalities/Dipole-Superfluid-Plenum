The plenum medium obeys a **magnetohydrodynamic** (MHD) equation with topological constraints:

Where:
- $\vec{B}$ = plenum flux field (magnetic field analog)
- $\vec{v}$ = plenum velocity (medium flow)
- $P$ = plenum pressure
- $\rho_B$ = plenum inertial density ($\approx 3.2 \times 10^{18}$ kg/m³)
